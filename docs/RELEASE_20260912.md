# September 12, 2026: acceptance follows actual use

The primary operating check now follows actual saved deliveries. It has no fixed
publication deadline or consecutive-day quota. A date without invocation evidence
is unobserved, not failed. The original 09:00 New York check remains an optional
timetable diagnostic; its archived results are not rewritten.

## What changed

- Delivery integrity, original automation provenance and news coverage are reported
  separately. Input dates, all six pairs, input archives, packet hashes and matching
  publication receipts still have to agree.
- Catch-up invocations now retain start/finish records and edition identity. A later
  scheduled check does not turn a manually generated edition into automated evidence.
- Legacy generation errors are read from matching saved drafts. Missing or mismatched
  draft evidence is identified without changing frozen text or receipts.
- The News page no longer treats a valid catch-up edition as failing a 09:00 requirement.
  Real dates, old-content notices and request warnings remain visible.
- The local operations report counts observed and delivered dates, with the earlier
  timetable diagnostic available in a separate disclosure.

## Verification

The complete isolated suite passed **748 tests**, with no failures or skips.
It covers missing dates, afternoon delivery, manual versus original automated
generation, retries, old generation errors, damaged records and mismatched hashes.
Desktop/mobile browser checks cover English and Chinese and preserve separate
morning/catch-up archives. Reports work offline using the site's fonts.

On September 12, four saved catch-up editions dated September 8 through 11 passed
delivery-integrity checks. September 11 had the new original automated invocation
chain. The preceding three editions remain historical delivery evidence, with
original automation provenance unconfirmed by that stricter ledger check.
Three dates contained generation failures; September 11 also retained rejected
drafts alongside one usable note. These observations do not establish semantic
accuracy, complete news coverage or long-term availability.

## Unchanged

Production model revision remains `2026-09-04.fold-local-cv-pca`, schema `1.1.1`.
Model settings, task triggers, request budgets and frozen attribution are unchanged.
No historical briefing was regenerated. Private interview notes are not published.
